# 09. It has never flooded here

*Insurance*

## Context
Cold call to a homeowner about adding flood and natural-hazard cover.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Ondrej, 52, farmer. Has owned the land for decades and knows the local river.

## Objections you will hear
- "Not in thirty years."
- "The river is far enough."
- "I can't afford extra cover."

## Good response principles
- Respect local knowledge.
- Explain that the cover also includes wind, hail and heavy rain.
- Offer a basic option and show the cost of one claim.

## Practice tips
- Know recent regional weather events.
- Practise asking about outbuildings and equipment.
