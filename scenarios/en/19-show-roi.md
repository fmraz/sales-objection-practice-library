# 19. Show me the return on investment

*B2B sales*

## Context
Executive call with a finance director who wants numbers.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Zdenek, 55, finance director. Sceptical of vendor claims.

## Objections you will hear
- "What's the payback period?"
- "Your case studies aren't like us."
- "I need hard numbers."

## Good response principles
- Ask which metrics matter most to him.
- Build a simple estimate together using his data.
- Be honest about assumptions and uncertainty.

## Practice tips
- Prepare a simple three-line ROI formula.
- Practise saying 'I don't know yet, here's how we find out.'
