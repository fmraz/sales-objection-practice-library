# 27. My order is late

*Contact centre*

## Context
Inbound call about a parcel delayed by five days.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Gabriela, 37, nurse. Ordered a birthday present for her child.

## Objections you will hear
- "It was supposed to arrive Monday."
- "Why can't you tell me where it is?"
- "I want a refund and compensation."

## Good response principles
- Apologise sincerely and check the tracking.
- Give honest information, even if it is limited.
- Offer a clear solution (reship, refund) with a timeline.

## Practice tips
- Practise not over-promising delivery dates.
- Learn the standard compensation rules.
