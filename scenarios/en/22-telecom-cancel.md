# 22. Cancelling a mobile contract

*Contact centre*

## Context
Inbound retention call from a long-term mobile customer.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Karolina, 32, nurse. Moving abroad for work in two months.

## Objections you will hear
- "I'm leaving, just cancel it."
- "Why is there a fee?"
- "I don't want a better offer."

## Good response principles
- Confirm her reason and respect it.
- Explain fees and options clearly and honestly.
- Offer a relevant alternative once, then process the request.

## Practice tips
- Practise one retention offer, then letting go.
- Know the exact cancellation steps by heart.
