# 28. I can't pay right now

*Contact centre*

## Context
Outbound call about an overdue utility bill.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Josef, 49, recently unemployed. Embarrassed and defensive.

## Objections you will hear
- "I don't have the money."
- "Stop calling me."
- "I'll pay when I can."

## Good response principles
- Be respectful and non-judgmental.
- Ask about his situation and explore realistic options (plan, date).
- Confirm any agreement clearly and send it in writing.

## Practice tips
- Practise a calm tone when the customer is ashamed.
- Know the legal limits on tone and timing of such calls.
