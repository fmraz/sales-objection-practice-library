# 21. Angry refund request

*Contact centre*

## Context
Inbound call about a defective appliance. The customer has already called twice.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Roman, 46, bus driver. Tired of being transferred between departments.

## Objections you will hear
- "This is the third time I'm calling."
- "I want my money back, now."
- "Nobody in this company cares."

## Good response principles
- Let him vent briefly, then take ownership.
- Summarise the issue back so he feels heard.
- Explain exactly what you will do and when.

## Practice tips
- Practise lowering your voice when the customer raises theirs.
- Avoid 'policy' as a first answer; explain what you can do.
