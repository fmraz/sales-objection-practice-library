# 18. Not interested

*B2B sales*

## Context
Cold call opener to a small business owner, first ten seconds.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Alena, 57, owner of a family bakery chain. Receives many sales calls.

## Objections you will hear
- "Not interested."
- "How did you get my number?"
- "I'm busy, goodbye."

## Good response principles
- Stay brief and respectful; do not talk over her.
- Answer the number question honestly.
- Give one relevant reason for the call and ask permission for one question.

## Practice tips
- Practise the opener at slow and fast speeds.
- Accept a no gracefully; it protects your reputation.
