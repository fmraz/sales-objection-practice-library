# 15. I need to talk to my boss

*B2B sales*

## Context
Mid-funnel call with a champion who cannot sign off alone.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Michal, 31, team lead in a finance department. Enthusiastic but not the decision maker.

## Objections you will hear
- "I have to run it by my manager."
- "He'll never approve it."
- "Can you send a proposal I can forward?"

## Good response principles
- Help him build the internal case rather than pitching harder.
- Ask who else is involved and what they care about.
- Offer to join a short call with his manager.

## Practice tips
- Prepare a short forwardable summary.
- Practise asking for a three-way meeting without pressure.
