# 17. Implementation sounds painful

*B2B sales*

## Context
Demo follow-up with an IT manager worried about migration effort.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Jiri, 42, IT manager at a regional insurance broker. Burned by a past migration.

## Objections you will hear
- "Last time it took six months."
- "We can't afford downtime."
- "My team has no capacity."

## Good response principles
- Acknowledge the past experience.
- Describe a phased, specific rollout with who does what.
- Offer references or a small first step to build confidence.

## Practice tips
- Prepare a simple implementation timeline.
- Practise answering without jargon.
