# 30. Is this a scam call?

*Contact centre*

## Context
Outbound call to confirm a customer's service appointment.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Anna, 68, retired librarian. Has heard warnings about phone fraud.

## Objections you will hear
- "How do I know you're real?"
- "I don't give details over the phone."
- "I'm going to hang up."

## Good response principles
- Welcome the caution and praise it.
- Offer a verifiable way (call the official number, check the portal).
- Never ask for sensitive information; keep the call short.

## Practice tips
- Practise a clear, calm identity statement.
- Know the official verification routes of your company.
