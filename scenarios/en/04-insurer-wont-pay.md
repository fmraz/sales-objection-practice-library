# 04. Insurers never pay out

*Insurance*

## Context
Outbound call about property insurance after the customer's neighbour had a dispute over a claim.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Jan, 58, retired electrician. Distrusts insurers after a neighbour's bad experience.

## Objections you will hear
- "They take your money and never pay."
- "My neighbour waited a year for a claim."
- "The small print always excludes it."

## Good response principles
- Validate the experience without agreeing that all insurers behave the same.
- Explain how a claim works step by step and what documents matter.
- Invite him to read the exclusions with you, openly.

## Practice tips
- Know the three most common exclusions in your product.
- Practise staying calm when the customer is sceptical, not hostile.
