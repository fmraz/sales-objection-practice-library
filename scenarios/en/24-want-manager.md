# 24. I want to speak to a manager

*Contact centre*

## Context
Inbound complaint call after a billing error.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Dagmar, 51, restaurant owner. Feels her time is being wasted.

## Objections you will hear
- "Get me your manager."
- "You can't help me."
- "I've explained this three times."

## Good response principles
- Do not take it personally or get defensive.
- Explain what you can resolve and what escalation will involve.
- If escalation is warranted, hand over with full context.

## Practice tips
- Practise a warm, non-defensive escalation sentence.
- Always write notes so she never has to repeat herself.
