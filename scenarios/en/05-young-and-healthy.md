# 05. I'm young and healthy

*Insurance*

## Context
Call with a young professional about life and accident insurance.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Adam, 26, software tester. Healthy, rents a flat, no dependants.

## Objections you will hear
- "I'm healthy, I don't need it."
- "I'll deal with it when I have kids."
- "I'd rather invest the money."

## Good response principles
- Do not scare; connect cover to his actual life (income, loans, sports).
- Mention that terms are usually easier when you are young.
- Respect the investing instinct and show how both can coexist.

## Practice tips
- Prepare one realistic, non-dramatic example.
- Practise asking about hobbies and income before suggesting cover.
