# 14. Not the right time

*B2B sales*

## Context
Call to a HR lead who postponed a training platform decision.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Barbora, 40, HR lead at a software company. Overloaded with hiring.

## Objections you will hear
- "We're swamped until summer."
- "Call me next quarter."
- "Priorities have changed."

## Good response principles
- Find out what is occupying her time and whether it connects to your solution.
- Test whether 'not now' is a real timing issue.
- If it is, agree what would trigger a restart.

## Practice tips
- Practise a calm 'what would make this a priority?'
- Note the date and reason in the call record.
