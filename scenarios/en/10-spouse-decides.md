# 10. My spouse decides on this

*Insurance*

## Context
Call to one half of a couple about a joint home insurance renewal.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Pavel, 44, warehouse supervisor. Defers money decisions to his wife.

## Objections you will hear
- "My wife handles that."
- "Call back when she's home."
- "I can't agree to anything without her."

## Good response principles
- Respect it and ask when both can talk.
- Offer to send a short summary they can read together.
- Agree a time for a joint call.

## Practice tips
- Prepare a one-page summary format.
- Practise scheduling a callback with a specific time.
