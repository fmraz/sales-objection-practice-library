# 07. Nothing will happen on holiday

*Insurance*

## Context
Inbound call booking a family ski trip abroad; agent suggests travel insurance.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Petr, 39, bank clerk. Optimistic, travelling with two children.

## Objections you will hear
- "We never needed it before."
- "My credit card already covers it."
- "It's just a week."

## Good response principles
- Ask what the card covers, calmly check the limits together.
- Use concrete costs of foreign treatment, not fear.
- Offer a simple option that suits a family trip.

## Practice tips
- Learn typical medical-cost examples for your destinations.
- Practise explaining a limit in one plain sentence.
