# 01. I already have insurance

*Insurance*

## Context
Outbound call offering a household insurance review. The prospect is polite but ends the call quickly.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Marta, 47, pharmacy manager. Has had the same policy for years and dislikes paperwork.

## Objections you will hear
- "I already have insurance, thanks."
- "I'm happy with my insurer."
- "I don't have time to compare."

## Good response principles
- Acknowledge it: having cover is a good thing.
- Ask what the current policy covers and when it renews before pitching anything.
- Offer a short, specific gap check rather than a switch.

## Practice tips
- Practise a first sentence under 10 seconds.
- Record yourself and count how many questions you ask versus statements you make.
