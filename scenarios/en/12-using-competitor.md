# 12. We already use a competitor

*B2B sales*

## Context
Discovery call with a marketing director who uses a rival CRM.

## Customer persona (fictional)
David, 38, marketing director at a mid-sized retailer. Satisfied but not delighted.

## Objections you will hear
- "We're happy with what we have."
- "Switching would be a nightmare."
- "Why would we change?"

## Good response principles
- Ask what works well and what he would improve.
- Never attack the competitor; explore gaps.
- Propose a low-risk comparison or pilot.

## Practice tips
- Prepare three neutral discovery questions.
- Practise hearing 'we're fine' as an invitation to ask more.
