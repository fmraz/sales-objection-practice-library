# 03. I need to think about it

*Insurance*

## Context
Follow-up call after a travel insurance quote was sent by email two days ago.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Eva, 29, marketing specialist. Interested but avoids commitments.

## Objections you will hear
- "Let me think about it."
- "I'll get back to you."
- "I want to look at other options first."

## Good response principles
- Ask, gently, what exactly she wants to think over.
- Resolve the real concern (price, cover, trust) instead of pushing for a yes.
- Agree a specific next step with a date.

## Practice tips
- Practise three different ways to ask "what would help you decide?"
- End every call with a dated next action.
