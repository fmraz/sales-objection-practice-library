# 16. Your price is higher than theirs

*B2B sales*

## Context
Proposal review with a purchasing manager who has a lower quote from a rival.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Radek, 50, purchasing manager at a manufacturing company. Trained to negotiate.

## Objections you will hear
- "Competitor B is 20% cheaper."
- "You'll have to do better on price."
- "Procurement says we need three quotes."

## Good response principles
- Clarify what is included in each quote.
- Discuss total cost and risk, not just the headline price.
- Trade concessions for something (term, volume), never give discounts for free.

## Practice tips
- Practise silence after stating your price.
- Prepare a 'what would you need to see?' response.
