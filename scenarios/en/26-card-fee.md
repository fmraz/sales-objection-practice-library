# 26. Disputing a card fee

*Contact centre*

## Context
Inbound call from a bank customer charged an annual card fee.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Filip, 28, freelance designer. Believes the fee was waived last year.

## Objections you will hear
- "You told me it was free."
- "I want the fee reversed."
- "I'll move my account."

## Good response principles
- Check the facts and history before answering.
- Explain the terms clearly; if there was an error, own it.
- Offer a legitimate option within policy and explain the next step.

## Practice tips
- Practise saying 'let me check that' and then actually checking.
- Avoid blaming the customer or the previous agent.
