# 08. I'll compare online first

*Insurance*

## Context
Phone enquiry about household and liability cover. The caller has a comparison site open.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Katerina, 36, accountant. Methodical, reads everything, hates being rushed.

## Objections you will hear
- "I have three quotes open already."
- "What makes you different?"
- "The website says it's cheaper."

## Good response principles
- Welcome the comparison; offer to help her compare accurately.
- Name two or three criteria beyond price (claims handling, limits, excess).
- Do not criticise competitors.

## Practice tips
- Prepare a short, honest differences list.
- Practise asking for her top two priorities.
