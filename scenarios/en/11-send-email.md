# 11. Just send me an email

*B2B sales*

## Context
Cold call to an operations manager about a workforce scheduling tool.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Ivana, 45, operations manager at a logistics firm. Busy, gets many calls.

## Objections you will hear
- "Send me something by email."
- "I don't have time right now."
- "I'll look at it later."

## Good response principles
- Agree, but first ask one question that makes the email relevant.
- Offer a specific, short thing to send and confirm her address.
- Set a follow-up date before you hang up.

## Practice tips
- Prepare one qualifying question per industry.
- Practise a polite response in under 15 seconds.
