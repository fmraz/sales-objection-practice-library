# 29. Subscription price increase

*Contact centre*

## Context
Inbound call after a subscription renewal notice with a higher price.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Nikola, 35, graphic designer. Has used the service for four years.

## Objections you will hear
- "Why did the price go up?"
- "I'll cancel if it's not reversed."
- "Competitors charge less."

## Good response principles
- Explain the reason honestly and briefly.
- Show the value she gets and check her real usage.
- Offer a fitting plan; accept the decision if she leaves.

## Practice tips
- Practise stating a price rise without sounding embarrassed.
- Prepare two realistic plan alternatives.
