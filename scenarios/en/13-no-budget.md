# 13. We have no budget

*B2B sales*

## Context
Follow-up call with a non-profit coordinator after a positive demo.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Simona, 33, programme coordinator at a charity. Likes the product, funds are tight.

## Objections you will hear
- "There's no budget this year."
- "We'd need approval from the board."
- "Maybe next year."

## Good response principles
- Find out whether it is no money or no priority.
- Understand how and when budgets are decided.
- Offer a smaller start or a plan she can present to the board.

## Practice tips
- Practise asking 'how do you usually fund tools like this?'
- Prepare a one-slide justification she can forward.
