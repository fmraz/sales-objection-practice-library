# 02. The premium is too expensive

*Insurance*

## Context
Inbound quote call for car insurance. The caller compares the quote with a friend's price.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Tomas, 34, delivery driver. Budget-conscious, drives 30,000 km a year.

## Objections you will hear
- "My friend pays half of that."
- "I'll just take the cheapest one online."
- "Why should I pay for extras I won't use?"

## Good response principles
- Do not defend the price first; compare like with like (cover, excess, assistance).
- Show which items drive the cost and which he can adjust.
- Offer options at two price levels and explain the trade-off.

## Practice tips
- Prepare a one-line explanation of excess versus premium.
- Practise saying the price without apologising.
