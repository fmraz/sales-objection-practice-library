# 06. I want to cancel my policy

*Insurance*

## Context
Inbound retention call. The customer received a renewal letter with a higher premium.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Lucie, 41, school teacher. Loyal for nine years, feels taken for granted.

## Objections you will hear
- "I want to cancel right now."
- "You raised the price without asking."
- "Another company gave me a better offer."

## Good response principles
- Listen fully and confirm you understand the reason.
- Explain the price change honestly, then check whether the cover still fits her.
- Offer real options; accept the cancellation gracefully if she insists.

## Practice tips
- Never argue about the letter; ask what she would like to see.
- Practise a calm, short apology that is not defensive.
