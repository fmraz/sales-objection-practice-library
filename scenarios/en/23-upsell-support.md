# 23. Upsell during a support call

*Contact centre*

## Context
Technical support call where the agent sees a relevant premium option.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Milan, 63, retired teacher. Calls about a slow internet connection.

## Objections you will hear
- "I called to fix a problem, not to buy something."
- "Why would I pay more?"
- "This feels like a sales trick."

## Good response principles
- Fix the problem first.
- Only suggest the upgrade if it solves his actual issue.
- Be transparent about cost and let him decide.

## Practice tips
- Practise a bridging sentence between support and offer.
- Skip the offer when the customer is upset.
