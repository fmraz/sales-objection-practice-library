# 25. Why would I switch energy supplier?

*Contact centre*

## Context
Outbound call to existing customers whose fixed price ends soon.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Vera, 60, retired seamstress. Doesn't trust offers by phone.

## Objections you will hear
- "I don't sign things over the phone."
- "I don't understand tariffs."
- "What's the catch?"

## Good response principles
- Explain simply and offer written details.
- Never pressure; give her time and a way to check.
- Answer 'the catch' honestly, including conditions.

## Practice tips
- Practise explaining a tariff in two sentences.
- Offer to confirm in writing before any agreement.
