# 20. The contract is too long

*B2B sales*

## Context
Contract stage with a clinic manager reluctant to commit for 24 months.

## Customer persona (fictional)
Dr. Renata, 48, clinic manager. Careful with commitments.

## Objections you will hear
- "Two years is too long."
- "What if it doesn't work for us?"
- "Can we do month-to-month?"

## Good response principles
- Understand the risk behind the objection.
- Explore options (shorter term, pilot, exit clause) within your policy.
- Trade term for price or onboarding, not for free.

## Practice tips
- Know exactly which terms are negotiable.
- Practise saying what you can offer, not only what you cannot.
